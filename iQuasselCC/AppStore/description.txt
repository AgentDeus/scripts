Name: iQuasselCC
Subtitle: Quassel Core Client

Quassel Core Client not a stand alone client.

iQuasselCC connects your iPhone to your own Quassel core. The core stays connected to IRC around the clock and keeps your history; iQuasselCC shows your chats and lets you take part from anywhere.

• Connect to several Quassel cores at the same time and switch between them
• Chat lists, networks, channels and private chats synced from your core
• Full chat history loaded on demand from the core, with marker line and read state
• Nick list with modes, away state and whois details
• mIRC colours and formatting, colourful nicknames, clickable links and channels
• Tab completion, input history and formatting toolbar
• Manage networks, identities, chat lists, ignore list, highlight rules and aliases on the core
• TLS encryption with certificate pinning
• Light, dark, Material, Solarized and AMOLED themes
• Notifications for highlights and private messages

Requires a Quassel core (version 0.12 or newer recommended).
